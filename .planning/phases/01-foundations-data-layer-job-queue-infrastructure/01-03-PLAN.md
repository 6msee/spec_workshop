<objective>
Finish the phase's data foundation and harden the one intake path the earlier plans proved: photos are
now accepted and stored safely outside the webroot, bad input is rejected with the project's standard
error envelope before anything is persisted, the remaining SPEC.md section 5 tables exist for Phase 2 to
write into, and reference data ships as an explicit placeholder rather than a blocker.

**User story (MVP):** **As a** reporter, **I want to** attach a photo of the fault to my report and get
a clear, immediate answer when something is wrong with my submission, **so that** the technician has
visual evidence and I am never left guessing whether my report was accepted.

Purpose: Phase 2's AI pipeline reads `ticket_media.storage_path` and writes `ai_classifications` — both
must exist and be correct now. Per D-02 the `buildings`/`assets` reference data ships empty or minimal
rather than waiting on the university's authoritative building-code list (SPEC.md 16 Q3), because
SPEC.md explicitly designs the system to work correctly with those tables empty. Redaction, the 24-hour
`_raw` purge, and rate-limit enforcement are deliberately NOT built here — they belong to Phase 2 and
Phase 4 — but the tables and storage paths they need are laid down correctly.

Output: migrations 004, 005, and 009, the placeholder buildings seed, media intake with MIME sniffing
and UUID filenames, full SPEC.md 6.3 error-envelope coverage, the AC-14 concurrency proof, and
`GET /api/v1/healthz`.
</objective>
